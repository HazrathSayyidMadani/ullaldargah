Frequently Asked Questions

What are the visiting hours?

The Dargah is open daily from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM for visitors and devotees.

Is there a dress code?

Visitors are requested to dress modestly and respectfully when entering the Dargah premises.

Can I make a donation online?

Yes, donations can be made through our secure donation portal. Please contact us for more information.

How can I participate in events?

Event schedules are posted on our Events page. You can also contact us for specific inquiries.
