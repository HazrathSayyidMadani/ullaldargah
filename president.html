B.G. Haneef Haji

B.G. Haneef Haji serves as the President of Ullal Dargah and plays a vital role in the administration and development of the institution.

Under his leadership the Dargah continues to strengthen religious, educational and charitable activities while preserving the rich spiritual heritage of Hazrath Sayyid Muhammad Shareeful Madani (R.A.).

His commitment towards unity, service and welfare has helped the Dargah continue its mission of benefiting thousands of devotees and visitors.

A sacred place of faith, spirituality, peace and blessings for devotees from around the world.
