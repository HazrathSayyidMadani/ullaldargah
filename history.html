About Ullal Dargah

A sacred sanctuary of faith, heritage, and community service, standing for centuries as a beacon of spirituality in Mangalore.

Introduction

Ullal Dargah is a revered Islamic spiritual center located in Ullal, Mangalore, Karnataka. It is a place of profound peace, devotion, and cultural significance.

The Dargah attracts devotees from all walks of life who seek blessings, solace, and spiritual connection. It stands as a symbol of unity, compassion, and the rich heritage of the region.

Our History

Origins

Founded centuries ago, Ullal Dargah emerged as a spiritual center for the region, rooted in Islamic tradition and local culture.

Spiritual Legacy

Over generations, the Dargah became a beacon of faith, attracting scholars, saints, and seekers of divine knowledge.

Community Service

Throughout its history, the Dargah has served the community through education, charity, and social welfare initiatives.

Present Day

Today, Ullal Dargah continues to inspire devotion, preserve heritage, and foster unity among people of all backgrounds.

Spiritual Significance

Ullal Dargah is a place where faith meets tranquility. It is believed to be a site of divine blessings, where prayers are answered and hearts find peace.

The Dargah hosts regular spiritual gatherings, recitations, and prayers, creating an atmosphere of deep devotion and communal harmony. Its spiritual legacy continues to guide and inspire.

Heritage & Community Service

Architectural Heritage

Timeless design reflecting Islamic and local architectural traditions.

Community Welfare

Charitable initiatives, food distribution, and social support programs.

Educational Programs

Islamic education, classes, and youth engagement activities.

Interfaith Harmony

Promoting understanding and respect among diverse communities.
