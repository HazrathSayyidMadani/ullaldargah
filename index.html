About Ullal Dargah

A spiritual oasis in Mangalore, Ullal Dargah stands as a testament to centuries of devotion, cultural harmony, and Islamic heritage. It is a revered center for pilgrims and seekers of peace.

The Dargah embodies the legacy of saints and scholars, offering a place for prayer, reflection, and community. Its architecture and traditions continue to inspire generations.
