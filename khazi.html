A. P. Aboobacker Musliyar Kanthapuram

A. P. Aboobacker Musliyar Kanthapuram is one of the most respected Islamic scholars in India and serves as the Grand Khazi, providing spiritual guidance and leadership to thousands of followers.

His teachings emphasize peace, compassion, education, unity and the preservation of Islamic values while encouraging harmony among people of all communities.

Through decades of dedicated service, he has inspired numerous educational, charitable and religious initiatives, strengthening the spiritual legacy of Ullal Dargah.
