Our Khazi

Spiritual guide and religious authority of Ullal Dargah, upholding Islamic traditions and community leadership.

Sayyid Abdur Rahman

Khazi · Ullal Dargah

A respected religious scholar and spiritual leader, the Khazi of Ullal Dargah is entrusted with the sacred responsibility of guiding the community in matters of faith, jurisprudence, and spiritual practice.

With deep knowledge of Islamic sciences and a compassionate approach, the Khazi serves as a source of wisdom and spiritual counsel for devotees and visitors alike.

Religious Duties

  • Leading congregational prayers and religious ceremonies
  • Providing Islamic guidance and legal rulings
  • Presiding over religious festivals and special occasions
  • Upholding the spiritual traditions of the Dargah

Khazi's Message

"The light of faith guides us through every challenge. Ullal Dargah is not just a place of prayer—it is a sanctuary of peace, where hearts find solace and souls find direction. May we always walk the path of compassion and unity."
